Fusion Drop — Privacy

Last updated 2026-08-01

What we collect

  • Local progress. Personal-best scores and achievement counts are stored in your browser's localStorage. They never leave your device unless you choose to submit a score to the leaderboard.
  • Leaderboard submissions. When you tap "Save Score" at the end of a run, your player name, your final score, the mode you played, and a small set of game-stat counters (drops, merges, level reached) are sent to our game server and stored alongside the public leaderboard. We do not collect IP addresses beyond what the server logs automatically; we do not associate your score with an account, email, or device identifier.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, third-party cookies, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • No location, contacts, microphone, or camera access.
  • No social-login or OAuth. There is no account system.

How to delete your data

Tap "Reset progress" on the start screen. This clears your local personal-best scores and any cached leaderboard data from your browser. Leaderboard entries that you previously submitted are not tied to an account and cannot be deleted individually — they're just rows on a public list. If you need an entry removed, open an issue on the public repository and we'll action it.

Open source

The game's source is available publicly. You can inspect, fork, and self-host. There are no hidden server-side behaviours beyond serving static files and the simple leaderboard endpoints described above.
